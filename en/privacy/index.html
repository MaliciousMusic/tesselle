Privacy

2026-11-24

In short

  • No cookies, no trackers, no analytics, no advertising.
  • No account, no form: the site asks for no personal data.
  • No resource loaded from another site: fonts, models and images are hosted with the pages.

What stays in your browser

Your composition is kept in your browser’s local storage (key tesselle.config.v1) so you can find it again. If the graphics card cannot keep up, a light display setting is kept for the session (key tesselle.lite). Nothing is sent. This storage, strictly necessary for the requested service, requires no consent. You can erase it by clearing site data.

The share link holds the composition after the “#”, which browsers do not send to the server. Anyone with the link can read the composition.

Host logs

To serve pages, the host GitHub, Inc. receives your IP address and technical connection data and may process them for service security, including in the United States, under its own policy: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement. The publisher has no access to these logs.

Legal basis and retention

The publisher retains no visitor data. Displaying the site rests on the legitimate interest of presenting the project.

Your rights

You have the rights provided by the GDPR: access, rectification, erasure, restriction and objection. Write to contact@tesselle.app. You may lodge a complaint with the CNIL, 3 place de Fontenoy, TSA 80715, 75334 Paris Cedex 07, cnil.fr.

The future product

This page concerns the website. The Tesselle system, when it exists, will have its own policy and an impact assessment.
