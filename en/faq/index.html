Frequent questions

What is Tesselle?

Tesselle is a modular living-surface project: a structure set inside the room carries 48 V power and data, and 500 mm tiles click onto it across floors, walls and ceilings. Each tile can become light, display, speaker, charger, sensor, heater or purifier.

Can I buy Tesselle today?

No. The project is at the design stage: circuit boards routed, firmware compiling, parts modelled. The first physical tile remains to be built and measured.

How much does an equipped apartment cost?

For the 58 m² reference apartment, the order of magnitude is €150,000 as a prototype and €56,000 in large series, ± 30%, excluding tax, installation and margin. These are working assumptions: the real bill of materials is not costed.

Do walls need drilling?

No, that is the principle of the system: the cage is designed to stand inside the room without drilling, and tiles go on without tools. Large ceiling spans remain to be validated. Anything involving water or 230 V goes through certified teams.

Are there cameras?

Not by default. Presence comes from a 60 GHz radar, a 32 × 24 pixel thermal array and floor load sensors, with no identifiable image.

Where does the home’s data go?

The system is designed to process everything on site. Nothing raw leaves; the rest is designed to go through a single filtered exit, with your consent.

What happens if a tile fails?

It is designed to come off and be replaced without touching its neighbours.

Is 48 V safe?

48 V is safety extra-low voltage. The system is designed to power only a recognised tile. In the shower zone the design drops to 24 V.

Is Tesselle a medical device?

No. No function makes a diagnosis and no medical use is claimed. The hospital floor shown is a layout study, not validated by any facility.

What does the configurator do?

It lets you compose a studio, a two-room flat, a 250 m² apartment, an office floor or a care floor, and read its estimated cost, power, yearly consumption and the uses it enables. Your composition stays in your browser.
