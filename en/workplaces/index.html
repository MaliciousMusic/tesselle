The floor you rearrange in a morning.

  • The wall is the screen, the room is the microphone Seamless pictures and voice understood on site, with a physical microphone cut-off.
  • Air follows occupancy CO₂ and presence drive filtration room by room.
  • Noise is masked where it bothers Tiles play gentle masking, booths and focus room stay quiet.
  • Know what is really used Anonymous occupancy and consumption per function, without badges or cameras.
  • Empty zones sleep Light, air and heat switch off where nobody is.
