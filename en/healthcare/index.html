A room that watches over, without watching.

A care floor covered in tiles: presence without cameras, a floor that feels steps, surfaces that disinfect, rooms that change between patients.

  • Presence and breathing without images Radar, thermal array and floor confirm each other. The patient is never filmed.
  • The floor senses getting up, light guides An amber path lights to the shower room, the nurse station is told.
  • A cycle between patients Continuous 405 nm, UV-C with the room empty and the door shut, smooth surfaces with no open joint.
  • The whole floor at the nurse station Occupancy, air, alerts and equipment read on one wall.
  • The corridor measures gait Steps and regularity are followed over time, with nothing to wear.
  • A room changes role Monitoring, rehabilitation, hosting a relative: tiles move, partitions don’t.
