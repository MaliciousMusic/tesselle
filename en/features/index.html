Features: goals and benefits

Sleep better

Goal
Understand the night with nothing to wear and no camera.
Benefit
You wake with light, you know what disturbed the night, the bedroom adjusts by itself.
How
Radar follows breathing and movement through the skin. Air and light respond.
Limit
Well-being only: no diagnosis.

Live in the right light

Goal
Follow the day and remove the switch.
Benefit
Light comes with you, warms up in the evening and guides you at night without waking you.
How
Variable warm and cool whites down to a night light, touch through any surface.
Limit
The translucent skin material remains to be validated.

Breathe air you know

Goal
Measure air room by room and act before you notice.
Benefit
Fewer particles when cooking, less CO₂ when sleeping or working.
How
CO₂, particles, VOC, humidity; HEPA and carbon filtration in the space behind the tile.
Limit
Filtration does not replace the building’s ventilation.

Hear and see everywhere

Goal
Make speakers and the television disappear.
Benefit
Sound comes from the room, the picture fills the wall when you want it, and nothing remains when you don’t.
How
Each skin vibrates; tiles play together on a shared clock.
Limit
Bass needs dedicated tiles.

Heat people

Goal
Put heat where people are, when they are there.
Benefit
Immediate comfort, empty rooms that no longer use energy.
How
Infrared films in the skin, presence confirmed by two cues.
Limit
Complements the building’s heating, does not replace it.

Be warned in time

Goal
See fire, leaks and intrusion coming.
Benefit
You are warned before damage, even away, even if the rest of the system is down.
How
Certified detectors on a separate circuit, sentinels behind tiles.
Limit
Certifications remain to be obtained.

Age at home

Goal
Notice a fall and a changing gait, without surveillance.
Benefit
You stay independent with more peace of mind, and relatives are told only if you chose it.
How
The floor feels weight and steps, radar confirms, light guides.
Limit
Daily help, not a medical device.

A healthy bathroom

Goal
Keep surfaces clean and dry without thinking.
Benefit
Less mould, less cleaning, a room that resets itself after the shower.
How
Antimicrobial glaze, continuous 405 nm light, UV-C with the room empty and the door shut.
Limit
Photobiological standard IEC 62471 applies.

Move and play

Goal
Turn the floor into a slate, a game, a coach.
Benefit
Children draw on the floor, adults train without equipment.
How
Touch through a thick skin, light pixels, load sensors.
Limit
Finger accuracy: about 2 mm in computation, to be measured under the real skin.

Change your mind

Goal
Move a function the way you move a chair.
Benefit
The place follows the stages of life, with no works and no electrician. A faulty tile is swapped.
How
Power and data at every node, identity checked on docking.
Limit
Water and 230 V are still installed by certified teams.
