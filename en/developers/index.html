Sell a home that keeps evolving.

  • Customisation becomes an order Each buyer choice is a priced tile or module, not a site variation.
  • A dry installation over the structure The cage goes up with no drilling or chasing. Only water needs a certified team.
  • Swap a tile, not a wall Each tile self-diagnoses and comes off without touching its neighbours.
